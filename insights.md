# Business Insights

## Revenue

- Certain product categories contribute significantly more revenue.
- Revenue varies across seasons, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

## Customers

- Some customers spend substantially more than others.
- Previous purchases may indicate customer loyalty.

## Payments

- A small number of payment methods dominate transaction volume.

## Marketing

- Discounts influence customer purchasing behavior.
- Subscription members generally demonstrate higher engagement.

## Product Performance

- Certain products are purchased much more frequently than others.
- Product categories differ in both popularity and revenue generation.
